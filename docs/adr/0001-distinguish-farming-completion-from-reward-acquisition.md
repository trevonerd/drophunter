# Distinguish farming completion from reward acquisition

Status: accepted

DropHunter distinguishes a campaign whose rewards are all acquired from a farming-complete campaign that has no reward the extension can still obtain automatically. Subscription-gated rewards and unverifiable Twitch-native badge/emote rewards remain truthful, visible remainders while the farming session and queue are allowed to advance. This avoids both false completion claims and infinite retries; the rejected alternatives were to mark uncertain rewards completed or to block automation until Twitch supplies evidence it may never expose.
